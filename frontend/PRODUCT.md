# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Individuals, small vendors, and freelancers who need accessible, cost-effective financial tracking and accounting assistance.

## Product Purpose
A cloud-based digital financial management platform serving as a virtual Chartered Accountant (CA). It aims to provide a scalable and intelligent alternative to traditional CA services, which are often unaffordable for the target audience.

## Positioning
An AI-powered, dual-brain CA platform that combines strict, authoritative financial analytics (text analysis) with autonomous receipt scanning (vision OCR) to automate bookkeeping for everyday users.

## Operating Context
Users operate the platform primarily as a web dashboard where they upload CSV ledgers or scan receipts, and chat with an AI wealth manager to get insights about their cash flow, savings rate, and burn rate.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Integrates Google Generative AI (Gemma for text, Gemini Flash for vision OCR).
- Backend runs Node.js/Express with Firebase Admin.
- Frontend uses React, Firebase Auth, and charting libraries (Chart.js/Recharts).
- The AI's responses regarding financial data should remain accurate and analytical, even if the overall platform tone is friendly.

## Brand Commitments
- Tone: Friendly, accessible, and modern.
- Existing brand constraints: The user will provide specific logos and colors that must be preserved. (Pending user input on exact colors/logos).

## Evidence on Hand
- Working Node.js backend and AI server.
- Python-based analytics engine.
- Existing React frontend scaffold.

## Product Principles
1. Make complex financial data approachable and easy to understand.
2. Automate tedious data entry (via OCR and CSV uploads).
3. Ensure the AI acts as a trusted, executive-level advisor while keeping the interface welcoming.
